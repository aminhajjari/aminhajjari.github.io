About Me

I am an AI/ML researcher currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Telecommunications Systems at Yazd University, where I also work as a Research Assistant at the CVPR Lab and the Wireless Networking Laboratory (WINEL).

My work spans deep learning, computer vision, federated learning, and trustworthy AI. I have hands-on experience with Python, MATLAB, and various ML frameworks, and I enjoy bridging theoretical research with real-world applications.

What I Do

I design and implement deep learning and computer vision solutions, develop interpretable AI models, and research federated learning for wireless networks. As a Research Assistant at the CVPR Lab and WINEL at Yazd University, I work under the supervision of Dr. Sadeghi and Prof. Abouei on AI-based systems and wireless network optimization. I also mentor students as a Teaching Assistant in Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Digital Communications. Previously, I led AI and deep learning R&D at Vira Fater Fartak, where I developed an intelligent tooth decay detection system, and completed an ML internship at IOTkaran. I also produce academic and research content for Danesh Academy on YouTube.
